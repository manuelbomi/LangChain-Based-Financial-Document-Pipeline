Northbridge Financial Group Personal Checking Account Policy (internal reference copy).

This document describes internal handling procedures for Northbridge Financial Group
personal checking accounts. Front-line staff should confirm identity using two forms
of identification before making any changes to account contact details. Northbridge
Financial Group requires a supervisor override for any fee waiver exceeding $50 in a
single calendar month. Account closure requests must be logged in the Northbridge
Financial Group case management system within one business day.

This is a fictional, synthetic document created for a portfolio demonstration and
does not describe any real financial institution's policies.
